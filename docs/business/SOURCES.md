## Top 5 numbers for a pitch (highest confidence)

1. **339 million weekly badminton players worldwide** (BWF/Nielsen 2018, 35 markets), vs 106 million tennis players (ITF 2024). Badminton's base is roughly 3x tennis. Caveat: the BWF page was not directly readable; the lower independent estimate is 220M (https://badmintonbites.com/how-many-badminton-players-are-there-in-the-world/). Pitch as "220-339 million".
2. **SwingVision charges Rs 19,900/yr (Pro) in India and $179.99/yr in the US**, and raised a $6M Series A (Oct 2023) with 100+ US college teams as customers. Proves paid demand for phone-video sports analytics. (https://apps.apple.com/in/app/swingvision-tennis-pickleball/id989461317, https://pulse2.com/swingvision-6-million-funding/amp/)
3. **Hawk-Eye costs $60,000-100,000 per court** (2011/2015 reports, dated); Elevate runs on a phone. (https://theconversation.com/beyond-the-line-call-how-hawk-eye-can-improve-performance-35962)
4. **Unit cost is tiny:** Modal T4 at $0.59/hour, R2 storage at $0.015/GB-month with free egress, Supabase Pro at $25/month. (https://modal.com/pricing, https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/, https://supabase.com/pricing.md)
5. **India: ~93% Android (StatCounter, mid-2026), sports tech $1B at 19% CAGR (Deloitte x Google), Bengaluru alone ~1.25 lakh monthly players on one booking app (Playo, 2025).** Note the Android share is from a search summary, not an opened page; the other two were opened. (https://www.deloitte.com/in/en/about/press-room/indias-sports-market-set-to-soar-to-reach-130-bn-by-2030-deloitte-n-google.html, https://theprint.in/ground-reports/bengalurus-badminton-boom-powered-by-techies-app-thousands-of-courts/2864587/)

## Planning cautions

- Freemium converts at only about 2.1% of downloads (RevenueCat, all categories); hard paywall with trial converts around 10.7%. India/SEA revenue per install is $0.06-0.09 at 14 days, far below the $0.44 Health and Fitness global median. Do not model Indian ARPU on US SwingVision pricing.
- Typical Indian coaching spend is Rs 2,000-5,000/month (2022-era sources); that is the anchor for a consumer price ceiling, not Rs 19,900/yr.
- Not found: India badminton player count, Foxtenn price, current PlaySight price, Machaxi fees, BAI/government academy totals, any SwingVision user count.
- Not opened (403 or unreachable): BWF pages, StatCounter, Sportskeeda, LyfSkills, CB Insights, Cosmos, Scientific American. Re-verify these in a browser before quoting externally.
